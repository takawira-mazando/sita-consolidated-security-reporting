Grounded AI Architecture

The 4-Layer Control Model: Only Layer 3 Holds a Model.

AI is a strictly isolated read path layered above the warehouse. The model never runs in ingestion or dispatch.

Layer 1 · Scope & RBAC Gate

Deterministic · Dual Axis

Tenancy subtree isolation (Where) + Role Catalog Authorization (What). Model cannot see cross-department data OR unauthorized reports.

Layer 2 · Fact Assembly

Deterministic · No Model

Pure SQL extraction. Tags facts into 3 typed disclosure classes (TENANT_SCOPED, ESTATE_WIDE, DERIVED). Zero free-text leaks.

Layer 3 · Model Residue

AIMI Africa · Only AI Layer

Gated reasoning: attributes global threat campaigns (AVP-3) and explains risk-delta movements (AVP-8). No DB handles, no tools.

Layer 4 · Verification

Deterministic · Two Gates

100% numeral fidelity check against fact pack. Strips unverified claims. Human analyst signs off before publication.

Legal Compliance & Privacy

Zero Raw Logs to AI: POPIA Protection by Design.

1. POPIA s.13 & NFR-AI-11

No Citizen Data Egress

Raw SQL queries, citizen IDs, employee logins, and source IPs are strictly blocked. redaction.py inspects and strips all PII before anything leaves the server.

2. Public Abstractions Only

Safe Threat Concepts

The AI only receives public identifiers: CVE IDs and MITRE ATT&CK codes (e.g., T1190). It reasons about hacker tactics without ever touching private data.

3. Resilient Fallback

Zero Production Downtime

If the AI provider is offline, the circuit breaker opens and serves the exact deterministic template. The platform never stalls or produces blank reports.

"We do not let AI count logs or invent numbers. Our engineered core computes 100% of the facts locally; AI provides external threat context and human synthesis."
